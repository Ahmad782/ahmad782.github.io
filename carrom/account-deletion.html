Delete your Carrom Pakistan data

App: Carrom Pakistan (com.mikao.carrom)

Fastest way — in the app

  1. Open Carrom Pakistan
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete my account and data
  4. Confirm

Takes effect immediately. No waiting period, no email needed.

Without the app

If you have already uninstalled the game, email hclodhran1@gmail.com with the subject "Carrom Pakistan — delete my data" and include your in-game player name. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

Because guest accounts have no email or password attached, a player name is the only way we can find your record. If you no longer remember it, tell us roughly when you played and we will do what we can.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and player profile
  • Your display name
  • Your rating, wins, losses, streaks and coins-pocketed totals
  • Your match history and any queued or in-progress match
  • Your in-game coins, friends list and friend requests
  • Your recovery code and notification token
  • Everything the game stored on your device

What is kept

Completed match records remain, but with your identifier removed so they no longer refer to you. A match belongs to both players — deleting it outright would corrupt your opponent's history and rating. Detailed per-shot logs are deleted automatically 14 days after a match ends.
