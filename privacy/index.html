Privacy Policy for Mikao Apps

Effective date: October 14, 2026 (previous version: October 8, 2026)

This privacy policy applies to all mobile apps published by Mikao on Google Play (together, the "Apps"). It explains what information the Apps use, why, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have, including under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). If an App has its own privacy policy, that policy adds app-specific details to this one.

The short version: the content you create or open in our Apps (files, documents, photos, notes, records) stays on your device unless an App clearly tells you a feature uses the internet or you choose to share it. Our free Apps show ads from Google AdMob, use Google Analytics for Firebase to see which features are used, and check Google Play for updates. These Google services collect limited device and usage data. Two Apps have online features that send only what the feature needs: Govt Jobs PK (job listings and ad scanning) and Carrom Pakistan (online matches, chat and voice chat). We do not sell your personal information for money, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

App-specific details

Everything above applies to every App. The sections below add what is particular to each one. HearLess and VaultPDF have their own policies: HearLess privacy policy and VaultPDF privacy policy.

Govt Jobs PK

  • Job listings: the App downloads job ads from our job server (hosted on Cloudflare). To show the right jobs, its requests include the province and education level you picked and any search words you type. These requests are not linked to your name or an account; our server uses them to answer and does not keep a per-user record.
  • Your preferences: your province, education level, saved jobs and alerts are stored on your device. Province and education level are also sent to Google Analytics as summary properties, and searches as search events, so we can see which regions and job types people need.
  • Scan a newspaper ad (optional): the photo is read on your device with Google ML Kit. Only the recognised text is sent to our server to find the posts and last date in it; it is processed for that answer and not stored. The photo itself is never uploaded.
  • Job alerts: the App checks our server for new jobs in the background and shows a notification on your device. No notification token is sent to us.

Carrom Pakistan

  • Offline play (practice, AI and local 2-player matches) needs no account and sends nothing beyond the ads and analytics data described above.
  • Guest account for online play: when you first play online, the App creates an anonymous guest account (a random account ID and a recovery code) on our game server, hosted by Supabase. You choose a player name; we never ask for your email, phone number or password.
  • Game data: your player name, rating, wins, losses, streaks, in-game coins, friends list, friend requests and match history are stored on the game server so matchmaking, leaderboards and friends work. Other players can see your player name, rating and leaderboard position. Moves made during a match are relayed to your opponent; detailed per-shot logs are deleted 14 days after a match ends. Reports you send about another player are kept to deal with cheating and abuse.
  • Chat: messages you type in a match are relayed live to your opponent and are not saved on our server.
  • Voice chat (optional): the microphone is used only if you allow it and switch voice chat on in an online match. Your voice is streamed live to your opponent through LiveKit and is never recorded or stored.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, your device's notification token is stored with your account so we can tell you about friend invites and turns. Tokens unused for 180 days are deleted automatically.
  • Delete your account: in the App go to Settings → Delete my account and data. It takes effect immediately. If you have uninstalled the App, see how to request deletion.

Rickshaw Rush

  • Your progress, garage, missions and settings are stored on your device only. There is no account and no game server.
  • The App shows ads (AdMob), including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and sends gameplay events such as rides started and finished to Google Analytics so we can balance the game.

Convertly

  • Every conversion, compression and edit runs on your device. Your images and PDFs are never uploaded; the App opens only the files you pick.
  • The App shows ads (AdMob) and sends feature-usage events (for example which tool was used) to Google Analytics. File names and contents are never sent.

FileRescue

  • Scanning, recovery and repair run entirely on your device. Your photos, videos and files are never uploaded.
  • The App shows ads (AdMob) and sends feature-usage events (for example that a scan finished and how many items were found) to Google Analytics. File names and contents are never sent.

Ads consent and your privacy choices

Our Apps use Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified consent management platform) to manage consent.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, you are asked whether you consent to personalized ads and to the storage of and access to information on your device (IAB TCF). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads, which still use some data for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. Where required, the App shows a US privacy message, and Google restricts data processing when you opt out.
  • Change your choice any time: open the App's Settings and tap Privacy options (or Ad consent). You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ads personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

EEA, UK and Swiss users (GDPR)

Legal bases

  • Consent for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for ads. You can withdraw consent at any time in Privacy options; this does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Legitimate interests for non-personalized ads that fund free Apps, ad fraud prevention, security, crash reporting and aggregated analytics to improve the Apps.
  • Performance of a contract for providing in-app purchases and features you request, such as job listings, online matches, chat, voice chat and notifications.
  • Legal obligation where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Data security

Data sent by the Apps, our servers and third-party services is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we choose established providers and keep data collection to a minimum.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling an App removes its local data from your device and stops all collection by that App. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID breaks the link to ad data collected before. You can also email us at hclodhran1@gmail.com to request deletion of any data associated with your app installation; we answer within 30 days.
