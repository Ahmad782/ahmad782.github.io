Privacy Policy for Mikao Apps

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile apps published by Mikao on Google Play (together, the "Apps"). It explains what information the Apps use, why, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have, including under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). If an App has its own privacy policy, that policy adds app-specific details to this one.

The short version: the content you create or open in our Apps (files, documents, photos, notes, records) stays on your device unless an App clearly tells you a feature uses the internet or you choose to share it. Our free Apps show ads from Google AdMob and may use Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics. These Google services collect limited device and usage data. We do not sell your personal information for money, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Who we are

Mikao is the developer and data controller for the Apps. Contact: hclodhran1@gmail.com.

Information we collect and how we use it

Content you create or open

Files, scans, photos, documents and other content you use in an App are processed on your device. We do not upload them to our servers. Some Apps download on-device models or language packs from Google; those downloads do not send your content.

Information you give us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message, and use them only to reply. If an App offers an account or cloud sync, it says so in the App and in its own policy, and that data is used only to provide that feature.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your card or payment details.

Ads consent and your privacy choices

Our Apps use Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified consent management platform) to manage consent.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, you are asked whether you consent to personalized ads and to the storage of and access to information on your device (IAB TCF). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads, which still use some data for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. Where required, the App shows a US privacy message, and Google restricts data processing when you opt out.
  • Change your choice any time: open the App's Settings and tap Privacy options (or Ad consent). You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ads personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

EEA, UK and Swiss users (GDPR)

Legal bases

  • Consent for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for ads. You can withdraw consent at any time in Privacy options; this does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Legitimate interests for non-personalized ads that fund free Apps, ad fraud prevention, security, crash reporting and aggregated analytics to improve the Apps.
  • Performance of a contract for providing in-app purchases and features you request.
  • Legal obligation where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because the Apps do not use accounts, data held by Google is linked to device identifiers rather than your name; we will help you with any request, and you can also reset your advertising ID or uninstall the App to stop collection.

International transfers

Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers are protected by safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses used by Google.

US state privacy rights (California and other states)

This section applies to residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other US states with comprehensive privacy laws.

  • Categories collected (last 12 months): identifiers (advertising ID, app-instance ID, IP address); internet or other electronic network activity (app and ad interactions); approximate geolocation derived from IP address; device information.
  • Sources: your device, through the Apps and the Google services listed above.
  • Purposes: showing and measuring ads, analytics, security, fraud prevention and fixing bugs.
  • Disclosure: to Google and its ad partners as described above. Allowing personalized advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under some state laws. We do not sell personal information for money.
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not collect it for advertising or use it to infer characteristics about you.
  • Retention: Google retains ad and analytics data according to its own retention settings (Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months). Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

Your rights

You have the right to know and access the personal information collected about you, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising, to limit use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To opt out, use Privacy options in the App or the Android ads setting above. For other requests, email hclodhran1@gmail.com; we may ask for information to verify the request (such as your app-instance ID), and you may use an authorized agent. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject.

Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security

Data sent by the Apps' third-party services is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we choose established providers and keep data collection to a minimum.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling an App removes its local data from your device and stops all collection by that App. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID breaks the link to ad data collected before. You can also email us to request deletion of any data associated with your app installation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be noted in the Apps or their store listings.
