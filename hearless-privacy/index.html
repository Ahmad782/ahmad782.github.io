HearLess Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy covers HearLess: Voice to Text (package com.mikao.hearless), an Android app published by Mikao. HearLess turns voice messages and audio recordings into text. It adds app-specific detail to the Mikao Apps Privacy Policy, which also applies.

The short version: speech recognition runs on your phone. Your audio and your transcripts are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and the app has no accounts. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects limited device and usage data. Unless you switch it off, the app also sends anonymous usage counts to Google Analytics. Neither ever receives your audio or transcripts.

Who we are

Mikao is the developer and data controller. Contact: hclodhran1@gmail.com.

Your audio and transcripts

  • Processed on your device. When you share or choose an audio file, HearLess converts the speech to text using a speech model stored on your phone. No audio and no text is sent to a server for this.
  • A private copy. HearLess copies the audio you give it into its own private storage so that it can transcribe it and let you replay it next to the text. Other apps cannot read this copy. The original file in the app it came from is not changed.
  • Stored only on your phone. Transcripts, any edits you make, Roman Urdu conversions and English translations are kept in the app's private storage. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • You are in control. You can delete a single transcript, only its audio copy, all audio copies, or everything, from inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.
  • Sharing is your action. Text leaves the app only when you tap Copy or Share and choose where it goes.

Voice messages often contain other people's voices and personal details. HearLess does not access your messaging apps: it only receives the specific files you choose to share with it or pick yourself.

Live capture (optional)

Live capture writes down audio as it plays in other apps. It is off until you start it, and Android asks you to confirm every time before capture begins and shows an indicator while it is on.

  • It captures the audio that other apps play through Android's playback capture feature. It does not record your screen and never opens the microphone.
  • Phone calls and internet calls are not captured, and apps can block their audio from being captured.
  • The captured audio is turned into text on your phone. The audio and the text are saved in the app's private storage, listed in History, and are never uploaded.
  • You choose which apps it listens to, you can stop it at any time from the notification, and you can switch the feature off in its settings.

Audio played on your phone can include other people's voices. Please only capture audio you are allowed to.

Speech model downloads

At launch the app also fetches a small list of available speech models from ahmad782.github.io. That request contains no personal details.

The app includes one speech model. If you choose to download a larger one, the file is fetched from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) over an encrypted connection and checked against a known checksum before use. This request contains no audio, transcripts or personal details; as with any download, the server sees your IP address. See the Hugging Face privacy policy.

Usage statistics

To learn which features are used and how quickly phones process audio, HearLess sends anonymous usage counts to Google Analytics for Firebase. These are tied to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or account, and include:

  • which screens are opened and which buttons are used (for example copy, share, Roman Urdu);
  • the spoken language and speech model chosen;
  • how long a recording was, how long it took to process, and whether it succeeded;
  • model downloads and Live capture sessions started or saved (not which apps were captured);
  • device model, Android version, app version and approximate country, which Google Analytics records automatically.

Your audio, transcripts, file names and contacts are never sent. Advertising ID collection and ad personalization signals are switched off for Analytics. You can stop usage statistics at any time with Share anonymous usage counts under Languages in the app. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and keeps it for up to 14 months.

App updates

When the app starts it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, using Play's in-app update service. No personal data is sent by HearLess for this.

Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy; see how Google uses data from apps that use its services and the list of Google ad technology providers. Apart from Google Analytics for Firebase described above, HearLess does not use any other advertising or analytics service.

Your advertising choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Languages > Ad privacy options in the app.
  • On your device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: optional model downloads and ads.
  • Notifications: progress of a transcription or download, and a notice when a transcript is ready. Optional.
  • Foreground service: lets a long transcription or download finish while the app is not on screen, and keeps Live capture running while you use it.
  • Record audio: requested only if you start Live capture. Android requires this permission for capturing audio played by other apps. HearLess does not listen through the microphone.

HearLess does not request contacts, location, storage or accessibility permissions.

Sharing and selling

We do not receive your audio or transcripts, so we cannot share or sell them. We do not sell personal information for money. Data collected by Google AdMob for advertising may count as "sharing" or "selling" under some US state laws; you can opt out through the ad privacy options described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because your audio and transcripts exist only on your device, you exercise these rights over them directly in the app. For data held by Google, use the controls above or contact Google. For anything else, email us.

Children

HearLess is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security and retention

Audio copies and transcripts are stored in app-private storage protected by Android. They are kept until you delete them or uninstall the app. Network connections use HTTPS.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
